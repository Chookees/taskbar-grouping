# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — reporting a bug, suggesting a feature, reviewing or writing code — should be able to do so without harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback on the work rather than the person.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are misunderstandings.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a decision may be "not now" rather than "never".

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments, including about someone's background, identity or ability.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Reach the maintainer through the contact details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/gianluca-schwekendiek). For behaviour that violates GitHub's own terms — harassment, spam, impersonation — [GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) goes to GitHub Support, who can act at the platform level regardless of what this project does.

Include what happened, where, and links if there are any. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

Deliberately no inbox address here: a single-maintainer project publishing one in a public file collects spam rather than conduct reports, and a channel nobody reads is worse than none.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may take any action they judge appropriate and proportionate, including editing or removing comments, issues or pull requests, and blocking a participant from the project.

This is a single-maintainer project with no separate appeals body. Reports are read and acted on in good faith, but there is no committee and no guaranteed response time.
